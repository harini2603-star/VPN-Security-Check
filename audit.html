🔍 About VPNs

🔒 VPN stands for Virtual Private Network.

🌐 It masks your IP address to protect your online identity.

🔐 Creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and the internet.

🕵️ Helps you stay anonymous and avoid tracking.

🛡️ Protects sensitive data on public Wi-Fi networks.

🌍 Lets you bypass geo-restrictions and access global content.

⚠️ Prevents ISP monitoring and potential data throttling.

🛡️ Security Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Keep software up to date.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Never share personal data over unsecured connections.
  • Always use a trusted VPN service provider.
